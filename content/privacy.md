**Last updated: August 14, 2025**

This website (https://stormacq.com) is operated by Sebastien Stormacq. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected when you visit this site.

# What we collect

This site uses [Umami](https://umami.is), a privacy-focused analytics service, to understand how visitors use the site. Umami collects the following anonymous data:

- Pages visited and referrer URLs
- Browser type and operating system
- Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
- Country of origin (derived from IP, but the IP itself is not stored)
- Time and date of visit

All data is aggregated and anonymized. No individual visitor can be identified from the collected data.

# What we do NOT collect

- **No cookies.** This site does not set any cookies. No cookie consent banner is needed.
- **No personal data.** We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or any other personally identifiable information.
- **No cross-site tracking.** Umami does not track visitors across different websites.
- **No fingerprinting.** We do not use browser fingerprinting techniques.
- **No user accounts.** This site does not require registration or login.

# Analytics provider

Analytics data is processed by [Umami Cloud](https://cloud.umami.is). Umami is open-source, GDPR and CCPA compliant by design, and does not share data with third parties.

# Third-party links

This site may contain links to other websites. We have no control over the content or privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

# Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
